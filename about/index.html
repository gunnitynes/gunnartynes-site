About

Gunnar Örn Tynes

Icelandic musician, producer and composer, and a founding member of múm.

In 1997 he and Örvar Þóreyjarson Smárason, with whom he had played in Andhéri, started múm. He has made records with the band since 1998, among them Yesterday Was Dramatic – Today Is OK, Finally We Are No One and Smilewound, and played more than 500 concerts in nearly 40 countries.

Alongside the band he produces, records and mixes for other artists, among them Ólöf Arnalds, Hjaltalín, Borko, Benni Hemm Hemm, Snorri Helgason, Skakkamanage, Mice Parade and Donata, and with múm has remixed songs by Goldfrapp, Emilíana Torrini and Sigur Rós.

In film and television he has composed for The Swan, Spark and Light, Chasing Birds and Summerlight… and Then Comes the Night, and worked in the music teams of scores such as Joker, Chernobyl, Women Talking and A Haunting in Venice, as a programmer, music editor and assistant.

He lives in Berlin.
