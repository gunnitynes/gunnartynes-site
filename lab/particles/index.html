Prototype · one continuous transformation
Nothing here is a video. Each cover is taken apart into 65,536 particles of light, one for every pixel, and your graphics card moves them all, sixty times a second. Between releases every particle travels to the place in the next cover that has the same brightness, so the darkest point of one record becomes the darkest point of the next.

Move the mouse, or drag a finger, through it.
