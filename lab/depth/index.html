Prototype · covers with depth
Each flat cover was taken apart on a Mac by Apple's own image-recognition, which finds the subject the way your phone lifts a person out of a photo. The subject floats on its own layer in front of the cover, with a real shadow behind it, and the place where it stood is filled in from its surroundings.

Move the mouse over a cover, or tilt your phone, and the layers part in true 3D.
