Playground

Take a song apart

The separate recordings of each musician in a múm song, opened up for anyone to rearrange, play and transform into sounds the song never had.

    Re-arrange

    Hold any player on any four bars, or move the whole band through the song.

    Cloud

    A granular instrument: the stems broken into grains and scattered into notes, drones and rooms.

    Forge

    Any moment of the band becomes an instrument: a sampler, strings it plays, or a pad of frozen grains.

    Textures

    Resonators, shimmer, tape and beat repeat on everything, and a sequencer that remembers every move.
